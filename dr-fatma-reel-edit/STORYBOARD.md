## The idea

1. **Problem addressed:** obesity + pre-diabetes / insulin resistance stealing basic daily function from a 55-year-old man.
2. **Misconception corrected:** that weight is a cosmetic number — here it decides whether you can tie your shoes, climb stairs, sleep or pray standing.
3. **Viewer insight:** a medically supervised journey (tests first, then 3–4 months) gave him his body back.
4. **"I didn't know that" moment:** at 55 he felt 65; after the journey he says he feels 45.
5. **Strongest curiosity gap:** «كان عنده أمل واحد بس… إن هو يعرف يربط الجزمة».
6. **Stopping statement:** the same line — a tiny, concrete, human hope.
7. **Loop bridge:** the closing «فدي كانت قصة نجاح تستحق إن احنا نعرفها لكم» flows straight back into «كان عنده أمل واحد بس».

## HOOK

**«كان عنده أمل واحد بس — إن هو يعرف يربط الجزمة.»** (source 15.36–19.48, moved to 0.00)

Why: the raw opening («الفيديو ده مختلف عشان إحنا هنحكي فيه حدوتة…») is a generic preamble. The shoe-tying line is the most specific, visual and emotionally surprising sentence in the take. It opens a curiosity gap (who? why only that?), which «ليه؟ لأنه كان عنده كرش كبير» answers in the next breath. Nothing is spliced. The hook is one continuous source range (s01, 15.23–21.63).

## LOOP

- **Sentence loop (Technique A/E):** the last line «فدي كانت قصة نجاح تستحق إن احنا نعرفها لكم» introduces a story, and replay starts the story: «كان عنده أمل واحد بس…».
- **Visual loop (B):** the A-roll crop eases back to 1.00× in the CTA, so the final frame and frame 0 share the same framing.
- **Motion continuation (D):** the CTA card collapses into a teal hairline at y≈1240. That line grows across the frame in the last 0.42 s, and at frame 0 the same line is full-width and retracts leftward over 0.5 s while the first caption lands.
- **Object loop (C):** the age ruler from scene 4 (55 → felt 65) returns in scene 8 (felt 65 → 45).
